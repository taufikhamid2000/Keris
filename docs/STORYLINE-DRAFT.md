# Keris: Malaysian history storyline (DRAFT, nothing built yet)

Status: discussion draft. No game code has changed. Every fact below must be checked against a named source before it goes into the game (list at the end).

## Premise

You are **Anak Arkib**, a young keeper at a forgotten archive. A heirloom keris leads you through the Kitab, and each chapter drops you into one era. In the Melaka arc you play a fictional keeper of the keris; in the other eras you are a fictional bystander. **Real people appear only as they are recorded**, and anything dramatised is labelled "Rekaan" (fiction) in the Kitab. The existing connected world becomes the **Lorong Masa** hub: each gate opens onto one era, and unlocked eras stay open to revisit.

Language: Malay by default, English in Settings (every line goes through one string table, so both languages ship together).

## Story thread

Each era the keris is hidden or passed on, and a diary page turns up in the Kitab. Ch.1: the cave, where people looked after one another. Ch.2: the Waris Keris line, ending with a keris smith's family caught in the fall. Ch.3 and 4: that family's descendants take part in the nation being built. Ch.5 and 6 close the loop in the archive room: the player decides what to preserve. The thread is fiction and is labelled so.

## The Melaka arc (chapter 2)

One keris passes from teacher to student as Melaka is founded, flourishes and falls. Everything about the keepers and their enemy is fiction and labelled *Rekaan*. Based on the owner's 2023 story idea.

**Names (confirmed by the owner):**
- **Waris Keris:** the line of keepers, handed from teacher to student.
- **Pakatan Bayang:** the enemy, a faction of traders and court schemers after control of the trade routes.

**Rules for the arc**
- **Hang Tuah** is a legend that people talk about, marked *legenda*. Hang Tuah is no longer the playable hero.
- **The Pakatan is wholly invented** and has members of many backgrounds. It never stands for a real community.
- **Real events keep their recorded causes.** The Pakatan is not shown to cause them. For example, Bendahara Tun Mutahir's execution stays disputed, as in the fact sheet.
- **The keris's power stays a rumour** inside the story.

## Mechanics

- Stealth and takedowns stay **only** in chapter 2 (all three parts).
- Chapters 3 to 6 use new verbs: collect, ask, read, choose. Choices change which testimonies you hear, not the real outcome.
- Peta, Kitab and Hikayat stay. Hikayat entries become "Sumber" (source) cards that cite where the fact comes from.
- Chapter 1 and the modern chapters have no fail state, only discovery.

## Schools vs general

Build for general players with a school-safe tone: no gore, neutral wording, sourced facts. Schools later add a teacher mode (progress, quiz export) and a syllabus map (KSSM Sejarah topics per chapter). That is extra work, so keep it as a later phase.

## Sensitive points

- 1969: no violence on screen, no reconstructed deaths, no blame language. Use official records and published scholarship, with the sources shown.
- 2020 and any recent politics: facts and dates only, no judgement. Consider whether to include it at all.
- No invented quotes from real people.

- The 1420s: who ruled, and contact with Ming China.
- 1511: exact dates and who commanded. Source: Sejarah Melayu plus Portuguese chronicles, noting where they disagree.
- 1955 to 1957: Baling talks, London talks, Reid Commission members and findings, Merdeka day events. Source: Arkib Negara Malaysia.
- 1962 to 1963: Cobbold findings, the Malaysia Agreement text, Singapore's exit in 1965. Source: Arkib Negara and the published agreement.
- 1969: election results and the National Operations Council. Source: official records, scholarly histories.
- 2020: dates of the resignation and appointments. Source: official announcements and major news archives.
