# Chapter 2 fact sheet: Melaka and the fall of 1511

Prepared 2026-11-06 for the Keris game (Malay default, English optional).

## READ THIS FIRST: limits of this research

### 2.3 Other legend-grade items

- Sejarah Melayu (Sulalatus Salatin) as a whole: the standard Malay source for the Melaka court, but it is a literary court chronicle, written later, with legend mixed into history. Treat its dialogue, omens and miracles as legend. (Per scholars quoted in WP-HangTuah.)
- Any "secret tunnel", "treasure", "royal keris with magical power" or "sword fight on the ramparts" scene is not backed by a source I found.

## 3. DISPUTED (give each version, or avoid)

**D1. Date of the fall of Melaka**
- 15 August 1511 (21 Jumada I 917 AH): WP-Capture infobox, citing Bosworth (2007).
- 24 August 1511 (Albuquerque takes full control after the second assault on 8 August): WP-Capture body; WP-PortMal body.
- "Mid-August / after about 40 days of fighting": search summary of mirrors.
- Safe wording: "in August 1511" or "between late July and late August 1511". For a game date stamp, the first assault of 25 July and the second of 8 August are better supported than a single "fall day".

**D2. Numbers**
- Portuguese ships: 11 carracks + 3 caravels + 2 galleys = 16 (WP-Capture infobox); "17 or 18 ships" (search summary of WP-PortMal); "18 ships, 1,000 men" (uncited line in WP-Capture).
- Portuguese men: 1,500 soldiers + 800 auxiliaries (Giovanni da Empoli, per Subrahmanyam and Parker 2008); 700 Portuguese + 300 Malabar (Albuquerque's Cartas); about 1,200 men (search summaries).
- Melaka defenders: 20,000 men, 8,000 guns, 20 elephants (Diffie and Winius, via WP-Capture, widely seen as inflated); only about 4,000 battle-ready (Albuquerque).
- City population: 100,000 (Albuquerque), 40,000 (modern estimate), 30,000 (Damiao de Gois), 10,000 houses (Ruy de Araujo). WP-Sultanate also gives about 2,000 for an early period, which is plainly not the 1511 figure.
- Safe wording: "about a thousand Portuguese-led men on some 16 to 18 ships" is within the range; or say "over a dozen ships". Never give a single exact number.

**D3. Death of Mahmud Shah**: 1528 (WP-Mahmud body), 1528/1529 (infobox). Say "about 1528".

**D4. Why Tun Mutahir was killed** (the Sultan's motives are literary and reported by later texts)
- Court intrigue, false accusation by Raja Mudaliar of plotting to take the throne.
- Mutahir's corruption, favouring his clan in appointments.
- A marriage dispute over his daughter Tun Fatimah (the Sultan wanted her).
- WP-Mahmud also says the Sultan realised his mistake and abdicated for his son Ahmad Shah. Ahmad Shah's fate is unclear (killed in 1513 per one reading). Do not state a single cause as fact.

**D5. When Melaka's ruler converted to Islam**: Pires says the first ruler (Megat Iskandar Shah) converted at about 72; the Sejarah Melayu places it under Muhammad Shah (r. 1424 to 1444); most historians say Islam was firmly established under Muzaffar Shah (1445 to 1459). (WP-Sultanate.)

**D6. Founding date**: conventionally c. 1400 under Parameswara; some propose earlier. Identity of "Parameswara" with Iskandar Shah is accepted by most but not all. (WP-Sultanate.)

**D7. Origin of Melaka's cannon**: Javanese (Barbosa), Pegu/Siam (Eredia), or unnamed (Albuquerque). One scholar (Wan Mohd Dasuki) argues there is no evidence guns were used in Melaka before the Portuguese attack, contrary to the Sejarah Melayu. (WP-Capture.)

**D8. Undang-Undang Melaka dates**: the PMC article quoted a practice period of 1422 to 1444; MCP/Liaw dates the core to the 1400s and others to the 1500s and 1700s; Winstedt (1953) is the classic article on the question (not read). Say "15th-century core, later additions".

**D9. A Famosa details**: demolition year (1806/1807/1811), who saved the gate (Raffles vs Farquhar), and what "A Famosa" refers to (whole fortress vs gate). The number of bastions varies in sources.

## 4. Game-use sections

### 4.1 Safe to state in-game (HISTORY, hedged as shown)

- In 1511 a Portuguese fleet under Afonso de Albuquerque attacked Melaka; the city fell in August 1511 (give month only). First assault 25 July, second 8 August.
- Melaka was a major port at the Straits; merchants from Gujarat, South India, Bengal, Southeast Asia, China and elsewhere traded there.
- Shahbandars (syahbandar) were harbour masters who managed foreign merchants, with four posts grouped by region of origin.
- Official ranks: Bendahara, Temenggung, Laksamana, Penghulu Bendahari.
- Undang-Undang Melaka and Undang-Undang Laut Melaka were the legal codes, a composite text with a 15th-century core.
- Sultan Mahmud Shah escaped, held out first in Pahang and then Bintan, later Kampar, and never regained Melaka. He died about 1528.
- Bendahara Tun Mutahir was put to death with his family on the Sultan's order shortly before the fall (cause is disputed; say "reportedly after accusations").
- The Portuguese built a stone fortress, A Famosa, from 1511 to 1512; only the Porta de Santiago gate remains, with Dutch "Anno 1670" mark from later repairs.
- The Portuguese held Melaka until the Dutch took it in January 1641.
- Islam was well established in Melaka court life and law by the mid-15th century.
- Tome Pires, a Portuguese apothecary-diplomat, wrote the Suma Oriental (1512 to 1515), the earliest detailed eyewitness-based description of Melaka.
- The keris is a blade of the Malay world with royal and ceremonial importance; wayang kulit Kelantan retells Ramayana-based stories (Hikayat Maharaja Wana).

### 4.2 Legend: must be marked "legenda"

- Everything about Hang Tuah as a person: his feats, duel with Hang Jebat (or Kasturi), Tun Teja, his death. Label: "Menurut Hikayat Hang Tuah / Sejarah Melayu (legenda; sejarawan berbeza pendapat)". Say historians debate whether he existed.
- The "Laksamana" office is real; a specific old laksamana in 1511 appears in Portuguese reports but his name is not recorded and linking him to Tuah is only a theory.
- Puteri Gunung Ledang and her seven demands.
- Saying "Takkan Melayu hilang di dunia" is not attested for Hang Tuah.
- Tun Mutahir/Tun Fatimah romance as motive: literary account, mark as "kata Sejarah Melayu".
- Magical keris, spirits, talismans.

### 4.3 Do NOT claim

- A single exact day for the fall of Melaka, or exact numbers of ships, men, cannon or elephants.
- That Hang Tuah was proven real, or that the Portuguese "Lassamane" was Hang Tuah.
- That the princess of Gunung Ledang, her bridges, or her demands were historical.
- That Hang Tuah (or any named person) fought in the 1511 siege or was at the mosque or fortress.
- That Mahmud Shah died in 1511, was captured, or that Melaka's sultanate "ended" in 1511 (it continued as Johor-Riau).
- That the keris "originated in Melaka", or that the keris is a Malaysian UNESCO listing (the 2005 inscription is Indonesian).
- That Pires gave a specific shahbandar scheme (not verified).
- That A Famosa was built from the Great Mosque with certainty; say "reportedly".
- That wayang kulit was performed in Melaka in 1511 (no source found).
- That guns/cannon definitely came from a named nation.
- Gruesome or sectarian detail, or modern religious politics.
- Any quote attributed to Albuquerque or Pires that I did not verify (including the "throat of Venice" line, which should be checked first).
